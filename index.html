Pertahanan defences

Musim padi the season

  1. Bajak: Traktor ploughs empty, stubble or dead petak.
  2. Isi air: tap a ploughed petak to flood it to Basah.
  3. Tanam: Mesin Tanam plants only Basah petak.
  4. Seedlings up? Lower to Sederhana: fastest growth, no pests.
  5. Gold padi? Drain to Kering.
  6. Tuai: Mesin Tuai cuts only Kering ripe padi.
  7. Tap the stubble for Jerami (straw).

Perosak pests

  • Burung pipit (sparrow): tap to shoo. Avoids Sederhana.
  • Siput gondang (snail): eats seedlings in Basah. Tap, +RM1.
  • Tikus sawah (rat): raids Kering padi. Tap, +RM5.
  • Bena perang (planthopper): spreads in Basah. Use Racun or lower the water.
